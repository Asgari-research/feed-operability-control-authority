# Sample form and compatibility

The parser-ready equilibrium file represents the published CALF-20 parameterization used for this focused P10 study. It is internally consistent over the declared pressure/temperature grid but is a deterministic reconstruction, not redistributed raw experimental coordinates.

The nominal process anchor corresponds to the structured/process CALF-20 VSA context and is used only as a calibration anchor. The 5/15/30% composition-condition metadata define evidence support but contain no pseudo breakthrough traces.

Material-form differences between powder, structured samples, laminates and packed pellets must not be silently merged into one experimental claim. External pilot files therefore remain outside the parser-ready input.
