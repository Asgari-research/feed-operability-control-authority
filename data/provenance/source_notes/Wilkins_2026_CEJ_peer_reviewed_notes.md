# Wilkins et al. 2026 — peer-reviewed pilot campaign

DOI: 10.1016/j.cej.2026.174238. The peer-reviewed article reports 75 pilot PVSA experiments (61 laminate, 14 packed) at 8, 12 and 16 mol% CO2. The open ChemRxiv SI remains the redistributed numerical source in this package; the CEJ paper is the authoritative peer-reviewed scientific record. Reported best laminate purity/recovery pairs are 95.2%/54.9% (8%), 94.6%/65.9% (12%) and 94.9%/72.8% (16%). These data support directional composition trends but are not a matched validation of the P10 LPP operability boundaries.
