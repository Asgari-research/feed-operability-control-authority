# Pereira et al. 2025 — independent CALF-20 uptake cross-check

DOI: 10.1021/acs.inorgchem.4c04727. Open article Table 2 reports CALF-20-SOL CO2 uptake of 4.65 mmol g-1 at 273 K and 3.38 mmol g-1 at 298 K at 1 bar, plus zero-loading Qst 39.09 kJ mol-1. These values are used only as an independent batch/source cross-check; they are not fitted with the nominal Nguyen/Liske thermodynamic reconstruction. The 273 K point is outside the nominal 303.15–373.15 K fit domain and must be labelled an extrapolation cross-check.

**Provenance correction:** The historical machine-readable source identifier `CALDERON2025_INORGCHEM_CALF20` misnames the first author. DOI 10.1021/acs.inorgchem.4c04727 belongs to Pereira et al. (2025). The identifier is preserved to avoid breaking existing source-data crosswalks; the bibliographic author/title have been corrected.
