# Dilipkumar et al. 2026 — high-fidelity CALF-20 process-validation context

**DOI:** 10.1016/j.cej.2026.177413

Peer-reviewed Chemical Engineering Journal study of CALF-20 in a parallel-passage TSA contactor.
The article reports multi-level validation of equilibrium, transport/heat-transfer and process-model
predictions against breakthrough/transient/cyclic experiments. It is used in P10 v3 only to document
the contemporary validation standard for CALF-20 process modelling.

No numerical CSS results from this TSA study are copied into the dry-PVSA P10 boundary validation,
because the cycle, contactor, humidity and regeneration mode differ. The P10 CSS boundary cases remain
a genuinely new validation task and are intentionally not fabricated or substituted.
