# Tang et al. 2025 — CALF-20 ZLC transport evidence

**DOI:** 10.1021/acs.iecr.5c02322

Role in P10: independent transport prior/context for the high-fidelity CSS model.

The package records the reported CALF-20 CO2 ZLC quantity `D/R^2 = 4.36e-2 s^-1` at approximately 0.1 bar and 311 K and the associated macropore-diffusion interpretation. analysis converts this to a spherical-LDF sensitivity prior through `k = 15 D/R^2`; this conversion is a **derived model mapping**, not an experimentally reported LDF coefficient. The N2 transport coefficient is an explicitly labelled derived Knudsen-scaling sensitivity prior, not a measured N2 kinetic constant.

These priors are not treated as point-specific transport measurements for the Nguyen-2023 headline cycle.
