# Breakthrough evidence status

The package includes verified CALF-20 breakthrough conditions near 5, 15 and 30 mol% CO2 together with independent kinetic scalar information. Exact machine-readable breakthrough traces are not bundled because verified numerical coordinates were not available from the sources used here.

Trace-level kinetic validation is therefore reported as open. An audited trace CSV can be supplied with `--breakthrough-trace-csv`; imported traces are retained with their provenance and are not treated as raw experimental data.
